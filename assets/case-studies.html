Case #4092 - Corporate

The Phantom Executive

Objective: Identify the source of leaked proprietary algorithms to a competitor.

Methodology: We deployed digital forensics to audit secure servers, combined with physical surveillance of key executives over a 3-week period.

Result: Discovered a compromised VPN endpoint and an executive meeting with rival firms. Evidence led to immediate termination and legal action.

Case #2214 - Fraud

The Invisible Inventory

Objective: Investigate a $2.5M discrepancy in logistics records for a global retailer.

Methodology: Forensic accounting coupled with undercover operatives placed inside the warehouse.

Result: Exposed a sophisticated embezzlement ring involving management and external trucking firms.

Case #3188 - Surveillance

The Ghost Shift

A logistics company suspected drivers were running unauthorized side-routes. We deployed GPS trackers and aerial drone surveillance. We provided 4K video evidence of the unauthorized stops, saving the company $400k annually.

Case #1105 - Background

The Fabricated CEO

A VC firm hired us to vet a founder seeking $10M. Standard checks passed, but our deep dive revealed a changed name, a string of failed LLCs in another country, and fabricated university degrees.

Case #5091 - Cyber

The Silent Ransom

Objective: Trace the origin of a ransomware attack without triggering data deletion.

Methodology: Reverse engineering the malware payload and analyzing cryptocurrency ledgers on the dark web.

Result: Identified the threat actor's location and recovered the decryption keys without payment.

Case #0992 - Missing Person

Operation Echo

Objective: Locate an heir who disappeared 5 years prior.

Methodology: OSINT, facial recognition across public databases, and field operatives deployed to three different countries.

Result: Located the individual living off-grid under an alias in South America within 4 weeks.
